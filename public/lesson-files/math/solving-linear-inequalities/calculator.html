Math · Inequalities · Tool

Step calculator

Type any linear equation or inequality. Use <, >, <= or >= for the sign. You make every move, the calculator does the arithmetic, and your work gets written down step by step so you can copy it into your notebook.

Same moves

Terms move, coefficients detach, the same as with equations. Tap a term to see what you can do with it.

Keep or flip?

Every × or ÷ asks you about the sign. Look only at the number you multiply or divide by. If it's negative, the sign flips.

Then graph it

Use an open circle for < or > and a closed circle for ≤ or ≥. Shade toward the numbers that work, and test one if you're not sure.
