Math · Equations · Tool

Step calculator

Type any linear equation. You make every move and the calculator does the arithmetic. Your work gets written down step by step, so you can copy it into your notebook or a Classroom answer.

Terms move

Tap a term to move it. The calculator does the opposite to both sides: added becomes subtract, subtracted becomes add.

Coefficients detach

Tap an x-term and choose Detach to divide both sides by its coefficient, or multiply by the flip if it is a fraction.

Keyboard

Type -3 or /2 or -2x, then press Enter to do it to both sides. Ctrl+Z undoes.
