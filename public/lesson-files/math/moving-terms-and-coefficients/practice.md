# Extra practice: move, then detach

Same calculator, but nothing here is recorded. Hints are free, and you can try a problem as many times as you like. Use it to get fast before the assigned work, or to go back over a problem you missed.

## Name the parts

```calc
title: Spot them

1. spot constants: 9 - 2x = 4x - 3
2. spot coefficients: -x + 5 = 2x/3
3. spot movable: 4(x - 1) + 6 = x - 8
```

## One move, then detach

```calc
title: Two-step

1. 3x + 4 = 19
2. -2x - 5 = 9
3. x/3 - 4 = 2
4. 8 - 5x = 23
5. -x - 6 = 1
```

## x on both sides

```calc
title: Both sides

1. 9x - 4 = 5x + 12
2. 2x + 15 = 7x
3. 6 - x = 2x - 9
4. x/2 + 3 = 3x/2 - 5
```

## Parentheses and like terms

```calc
title: Unlock and combine

1. 2(x + 6) = 3x - 1
2. 5(x - 2) = 20
3. -(x - 3) = 8
4. 3x + 4 - x = x + 10
5. 2x + 5 = 2x + 9
   > The x-terms cancel and you are left with 5 = 9. That is never true, so there is no solution.
```
